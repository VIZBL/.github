# Vizbl

**Product visualization for e-commerce.** Vizbl lets shoppers see products in their own space and on their own photos, using 3D, AR and AI. Merchants add it to a store with a widget, an SDK or an API.

[Website](https://vizbl.com) · [Developer docs](https://developer.vizbl.com) · [Help center](https://help.vizbl.com) · [info@vizbl.com](mailto:info@vizbl.com)

## Products

| Product | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Core AR & 3D | 3D viewer and AR placement for any product |
| Rugs | Shows a rug in a room photo or on the shopper's floor |
| Flooring | Previews flooring in a room |
| Wall Art | Places posters and art on a wall |
| AI Photo Try-On | Tries a product on the shopper's own photo |
| AutoFit AI | Wheel try-on for car dealers |
| Showroom AR | iPad catalog for showroom and field sales teams |

## Get started

1. Create an account at [vizbl.com](https://vizbl.com). New accounts get 1,000 free tokens, no card required.
2. Add a product and get its Object ID.
3. Show it in your store with a widget, or in your app with an SDK.
